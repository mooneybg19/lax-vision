Fit

Place 4 landmarks to fit the field.

    Paint intersections are the most precise points: where box and restraining lines meet, line ends, crease edges, and on football fields where yard lines cross the sidelines or the hash marks. Click the middle of the paint. Once four or more points give a fit, the tool snaps the field onto the painted lines and checks how much of it matches. With faint paint, add a fifth point so your points can check each other. You can only save a checked fit, and the magenta field should sit right on the lines before you save. Skip close-ups, crowd shots and replays.

    Enter save and next, S skip, Z undo point, Esc drop selection, arrow keys move between frames. Drag any point to adjust it.
